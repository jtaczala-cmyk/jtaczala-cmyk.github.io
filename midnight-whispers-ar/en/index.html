Before you start

Important safety information

Midnight Whispers AR is an augmented reality game. You see the world through your phone camera and turn around, so it is easy to lose track of what is really happening around you.

  • Look around before you start. Only play in a safe, open place with plenty of free space around you.
  • Watch out for obstacles: furniture, walls, windows, stairs, kerbs, other people and animals.
  • Never play in the street, near traffic, railway tracks, water or at heights.
  • Do not play while driving, cycling, riding a scooter or walking in a crowd.
  • Photosensitive epilepsy warning. The game contains flashes, flickering and screen shake. People with photosensitive epilepsy or sensitivity to flashing lights should not play. If you feel dizzy, nauseous or your eyes get tired, stop playing immediately.
  • Hold your phone firmly. Children should play under adult supervision.
  • Age restriction (13+): the game is intended for people aged 13 and over (in countries with a higher age threshold, e.g. 16+, and for younger children – only with the consent and under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian).
  • Respect the law and other people's property. Do not enter private property, restricted areas or dangerous places.
  • Camera privacy: the camera feed is processed locally on your device only – nothing is recorded, stored or sent over the internet.
⚠️ Important – safety: Always be aware of your surroundings. Do not play in the street, near traffic or while driving. You use the game at your own risk. Midnight Whispers AR

A whisper in the dark that quickly turns into a scream.
Look around. They're already whispering behind you.

  • Turn around with your phone – the radar in the corner shows where they are coming from.
  • Tap a creature or press FIRE to hit the target in the middle of the crosshair.
  • Red glow at the edge = something is right next to you!
  • Sound: after you start you will hear a short chime. Hear nothing? Turn up the volume and tap the screen.
  • In simulation: drag with your finger/mouse or use the arrow keys / WASD, space = fire.
