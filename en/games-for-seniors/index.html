Free games for seniors

Here you'll find free games that are easy to pick up, even if you don't play games very often. The text is large, the buttons are big, and no adverts ever pop up in the middle of a game.

There is nothing to download and no account to create. Tap a game and it starts straight away in your browser – on a tablet, phone or computer.

  • Easy to see: big pictures, clear colours and strong contrast.
  • Easy to tap: large buttons, so your finger lands where you mean it to.
  • No ads and no purchases: nothing pops up and nothing asks for payment.
  • No account or password: open the page and play.
  • Short rounds: take a break whenever you like and carry on later.

Which game should I choose?

For something completely relaxed, start with Grandma's Garden or Grandma's Cookie Tin: you combine matching things and there's no clock hurrying you. If you like numbers, try Pure Logic – a classic number puzzle with a new daily puzzle. Hefau is a board game you can play with your grandchildren on one phone.

Sock Drawer has a countdown clock, so it suits the moments when you fancy a bit of pace.

Tip: put the game on your home screen

On a tablet or phone you can add a game as an icon on your home screen. Open the game, tap your browser's menu and choose “Add to Home Screen”. Next time it's just one tap.

Questions and answers

Are the games really free?

Yes. All the games are free, with no ads and no in-game purchases.

Do I need to sign up?

No. You need no account, no email and no password. Just tap a game.

Which game is easiest to start with?

Grandma's Garden or Grandma's Cookie Tin. You combine two matching things and there's no countdown.

Can I play on a tablet?

Yes. The games work on tablets, phones and computers, right in the browser.

What if I tap the wrong thing?

No harm done. You can always start again, and nothing is ever bought or sent anywhere.
