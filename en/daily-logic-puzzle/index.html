Daily logic puzzle

Pure Logic has one shared puzzle every day — everyone who plays today gets the same board.

Play today's puzzle

How to play

  1. Tap an empty cell, then tap a digit from 1 to 9.
  2. No digit may repeat in any row, column or 3×3 box.
  3. Use notes for pencil marks, and undo if you change your mind. You get 3 hints per board.
  4. Finished? Share your result and come back tomorrow for a new board.

Want something faster? Quick 60-second games

There is no luck involved. Every board can be solved with logic alone, step by step. The daily board is medium difficulty and usually takes 5–15 minutes.

When you finish, you can copy a short emoji result and send it without spoiling the answer. Solve a board every day to build a streak. It is free, with no ads, no app and no account.

Questions and answers

Is the daily logic puzzle free?

Yes. Pure Logic is free in your browser. There are no ads, no in-game purchases and no account. You do not download an app.

Is it the same puzzle for everyone?

Yes. The daily board is the same for everyone that day. Tomorrow there is a new one.

How long does it take?

The daily board is medium difficulty, usually 5–15 minutes. Easy boards take about 2–5 minutes and hard boards 15–30 minutes.

Can I share my result?

Yes. When you finish, you can copy an emoji result that shows your time and streak without giving away the answer.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It works on phones, tablets and computers, with big buttons. On a computer you can also type the digits.
