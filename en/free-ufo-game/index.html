How to play

  1. On a phone: put one finger near the bottom of the screen and slide sideways. The cannon follows your finger and fires while you hold it down.
  2. On a computer: arrow keys or A/D move, and Space fires. In the menu you can switch on buttons instead of dragging.
  3. Your cannon has only one shot in the air at a time, so make it count. Clear the aliens at the outer edges first, and take the shot when the Red Manta flies over.
