Cat games with Magic the cat

Magic is Grandma's cat, and he gets everywhere: the sock drawer, the yarn basket, the cookie tin and the garden. He makes a mess, swipes things with his paw and looks just as innocent every time.

Here are all the games where Magic plays the lead. They are free, run right in your browser and have no ads and no account.

Who is Magic?

Magic is a curious cat who lives with Grandma and Grandpa Jørgen. He loves balls of yarn, socks and anything that can be nudged off a table. When Grandpa Jørgen drops a cookie, Magic has a paw on it before it hits the floor.

What does Magic do in the games?

  • In Sock Drawer he has scattered all the socks, and you have to find the pairs.
  • In Grandma's Yarn he drops yarn into the basket, and matching things join together.
  • In Grandma's Cookie Tin and Grandma's Garden he watches and cheers – and happily steals something.
  • In Kometknus he flies a Christmas sleigh between snowy comets.

For cat lovers big and small

The games suit children and adults alike. There is no violence and no chat, and every game is available in English, Norwegian and Polish – handy when grandparents and grandchildren want to play together.

Questions and answers

Are the cat games free?

Yes. All the games are free, with no ads and no in-game purchases.

Do I need to download an app?

No. You play right in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

Which cat game is best for children?

Grandma's Garden and Grandma's Cookie Tin are calm and have no clock. Sock Drawer is a little faster.

What is the cat's name?

The cat is called Magic, and he lives with Grandma and Grandpa Jørgen.
