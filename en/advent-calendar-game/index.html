How to play

  1. Open Kitty Yarn in your browser. You do not need an account or an app.
  2. Drag with one thumb to aim, and let go so Magic drops the yarn.
  3. Merge matching items into bigger ones – all the way to the reindeer sweater.
  4. Play today's basket every day in December and open a new window in the advent calendar.

The game is called Kitty Yarn. Magic the cat drops yarn into grandma's basket, and two matching items merge into a bigger one: thimble, button, yarn ball, pompom hat, scarf, mittens and finally a reindeer Christmas sweater. Today's basket has 40 throws that are the same for everyone, and every finished basket in December hangs a new ornament on grandma's tree.

Questions and answers

How does the advent calendar in the game work?

From 1 to 24 December you open the day's window by playing today's basket in Kitty Yarn. Each window hangs a new ornament on grandma's Christmas tree.

Is the game free, with no ads?

Yes. Kitty Yarn is free, with no ads, no account and no app. You play right in your browser.

What is today's basket?

Today's basket is 40 throws that are the same for everyone on the same day. Only the first attempt counts, and you can share your result as emoji with friends.

How do I play on a phone?

Drag with one thumb to aim and let go so Magic the cat drops the yarn. Two matching items merge into a bigger one, all the way to the reindeer sweater.
