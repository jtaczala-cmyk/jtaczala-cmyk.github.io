Cozy games in your browser

Sometimes you just want something calm to do. These cozy games take place at Grandma's house: you bake, tend the garden, tidy the pantry shelf and hunt for the socks the cat has dragged all over the place.

Everything is free and runs right in your browser. No ads, no account and no stress – just a little comfort, whether you've got five minutes or a whole evening.

What makes a game cozy?

A cozy game isn't about winning fast – it's about enjoying the way there. There's no violence and no scary surprises, and most of the games here have no countdown at all. You combine matching things, sort things out and watch something grow – cookies in the tin, flowers in the garden, jars holding a single kind of fruit.

Meet the household

  • Grandma runs the kitchen and the garden, and there is always something nice in the cookie tin.
  • Grandpa Jørgen is warm-hearted and a bit clumsy: he trips over the ball of yarn, looks for the glasses perched on his forehead and knocks over the coffee flask.
  • Magic the cat makes a mess, swipes things with a paw and looks completely innocent afterwards.

When do they fit best?

With a cup of coffee, on the bus, on the sofa after dinner or as a short break from screen work. Grandma's Cookie Tin, Grandma's Preserves and Grandma's Yarn have a daily puzzle that's the same for everyone, so you can compare with your family. Sock Drawer and Grandma's Gingerbread are a little livelier when you want a small challenge.

Questions and answers

Are these cozy games free?

Yes. All the games are free, with no ads and no in-game purchases.

Do I need to download anything?

No. You play right in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer. No app and no account.

Are the games stressful?

No, most of them have no clock. Sock Drawer has a countdown and Grandma's Gingerbread is about timing, so those two are a bit livelier.

Are they suitable for children?

Yes. There is no violence and no chat with strangers, and the characters are friendly and funny.

Are the games available in other languages?

Yes, every game is available in English, Norwegian and Polish.
