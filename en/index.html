Stop. Think. Work safe – in 60 seconds.

A game that supports health and safety on construction sites.

Mission

A short game about working safely

Stop60 is a series of short mobile games about work where health and safety rules are a key part of working safely. The game aims to remind players of these rules through short slogans and information shown during play. It can help raise awareness of risks at work, but it does not replace health and safety training or workplace instructions.

What it is

How it supports health and safety

01

Short safety reminders

Each 60-second round shows 3–4 short safety slogans.

02

For every trade

Carpenters, electricians, plumbers, plant operators, apprentices – the reminders apply to everyone on site.

03

A quick refresher

One minute before or after work, or during a break.

04

A conversation starter

Play a round at a toolbox talk or morning meeting, then talk about the reminders.

Examples from the game:

  • Stop if in doubt
  • Lock out, tag out
  • Test before touch
  • H&S – PPE on
  • Near miss – report it

Stop60 does not replace EWR training, Health & Safety induction or your company's own procedures.
