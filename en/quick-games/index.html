Quick games for a short break

Got a minute? These games start fast and end fast. No sign-up, no download and no ads – open the page and play.

Start with Sneaky Bugs. Night falls over the truck park and black bugs with glowing red eyes come out. You have one truck and 60 seconds. Drive into the swarm, squash bugs and stay away from their venom.

Every 6 bugs unlock a new skill, and every 5 bugs add an extra second to the clock. After the round you can copy an emoji result and challenge a friend.

Play Sneaky Bugs

How Sneaky Bugs works

  1. Hold your finger (or the mouse button) where the truck should drive.
  2. Squash bugs while you move. Waves grow across 5 zones of the map.
  3. Use the skill buttons: repair, turbo, crush, armour and the horn.
  4. Note: the game has flashes and explosions.

Questions and answers

Are these games free with no ads?

Yes. All games here are free in your browser. There are no ads, no in-game purchases and no account.

How long is a round?

A round of Sneaky Bugs lasts 60 seconds plus any bonus time you earn. Ufobang and Redning also have short rounds.

Do they work on a phone?

Yes. They are made for phones and also work on a computer.

Is there a leaderboard?

Sneaky Bugs has an optional online leaderboard. You can add a nickname if you want (16+), or just play.
