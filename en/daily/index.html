Today’s challenge

Every day there is a new board — the same one for everyone. Play it in your browser, free and with no ads. When you finish, you can challenge someone you meet on the way to work, or at work.

Play today’s challenge

How it works

  1. Open today’s board and fill in the numbers 1 to 9. No digit repeats in a row, a column or a 3×3 box.
  2. When the board is solved, you get a score out of 5 and a button labelled Challenge…
  3. Pick who to challenge — your boss, the Prime Minister, your accountant, a coworker, the bus driver, a neighbour, the kiosk seller, your manager, the intern, or someone else.
  4. Your phone opens the share sheet, or the text is copied. Nothing is sent on its own. The link points here: stop60.no/daily/

Who can you challenge?

Take your pick. The lines are short and light-hearted, with no real names. The other person gets your result and the link to today’s challenge.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Today’s challenge is free in your browser. No ads, no account, and no app to install.

Is it the same board for everyone?

Yes. Everyone who plays today gets the same board. A new one arrives tomorrow.

Does the site send the message for me?

No. You choose whether to share or paste the text. Nothing goes out on its own.
