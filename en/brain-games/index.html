Questions

Are these free brain games online, with no download and no ads?

Yes. The games on this page are free in the browser. There are no ads, no in-game purchases and no account. You do not download an app.

Are there brain games for seniors, free to play?

This page uses large text and large buttons. Pure Logic is a quiet number puzzle with a daily board, Dagens brett, the same board for everyone that day. Some of the other games are steered by tilting the phone, and some are faster. You choose.

Can children play?

Yes. The games are free for everyone, including children, and they need no account. The first ones are calm. Ufobang, Star Storm and Redning are shorter and faster.

Can I play on a short break?

Yes. You open a game in the browser and stop when you want. Pure Logic has short rounds and one daily board, Dagens brett.

What is Dagens brett?

In Pure Logic, Dagens brett is the daily board. It is the same for everyone that day. You fill in the digits 1–9 so none repeats in a row, column or 3×3 box.
