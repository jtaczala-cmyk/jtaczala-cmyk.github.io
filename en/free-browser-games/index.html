Free browser games, no ads

Stop60 is a small collection of free browser games with no ads. You play in the tab you already have open, on a phone or a computer. There is no account, no download and nothing to buy in the games.

A lot of free game sites make you wait through advertising before you can start. Here you open a game and play. If you only have a minute, that is enough. If you want a reason to come back tomorrow, the daily puzzle and the daily run are waiting — the same challenge for everyone that day.

The games are in English, Norwegian and Polish. All ten are listed below, with today’s games first.

Questions

Are these really free browser games with no ads?

Yes. Stop60 does not charge, does not show ads and does not ask you to create an account. You open the page and play.

Do I have to install an app?

No. Every game runs in the browser on a phone or a computer.

What are the daily puzzle and the daily run?

In Pure Logic, everyone gets the same number board that day. In Lava Marble, the daily run is the same course for everyone. When you finish, you can share the result without spoiling the answer, and come back tomorrow to keep a streak.

Which languages are available?

Most games are in English, Norwegian and Polish. Sneaky Bugs is in Polish and English.
