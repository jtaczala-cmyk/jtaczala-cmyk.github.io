Nøstekurv · free puzzle game

Privacy

Last updated 21.11.2026 · Norsk · Polski

In short

Nøstekurv has no accounts, no ads and no profiling, and we sell no data. The game uses no cookies. Anyone can play in full without the leaderboard.

Controller

Jacek Taczala (a private person who made the game, free for everyone), contact: kontakt@stop60.no.

Data on your device

Your browser storage (localStorage) on this device keeps your language, sound and vibration choices your best score, today's result, your streak and the advent calendar (key nostekurv.v1); if you use the leaderboard, also your nickname and leaderboard choice. These are not cookies and do not leave the device, except what the leaderboard needs (below).

Online leaderboard (optional)

The leaderboard is off until you turn it on. Only if you type a nickname and tick the box are your nickname, score, biggest item (in the daily basket: the day number), number of merges and play time sent to the public leaderboard (a database at Supabase). Do not use your real name or email – a made-up nickname is enough. To stop cheating, the server stores a random one-time ID with the start time, and for up to 24 hours a salted hash of the IP address as spam protection. Legal basis: consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); spam protection relies on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). To have a nickname removed, write to kontakt@stop60.no.

Anonymous statistics (GoatCounter)

We count visits and the events “game started”, “game over”, “daily basket finished” and “game shared” with GoatCounter, without cookies. IP address and browser are used only in server memory (up to 8 hours) to count unique visits; only totals are stored. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). You can opt out: block scripts, or open the game with #toggle-goatcounter at the end of the address.

Recipients

GoatCounter (statistics, servers in the EU). Supabase Inc. – leaderboard database. GitHub, Inc. (USA) – hosting via GitHub Pages; GitHub may log IP address and browser data when pages load (EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

Your rights

You have the right of access, correction, deletion, restriction and to object. Write to kontakt@stop60.no. You can complain to Datatilsynet or UODO.
