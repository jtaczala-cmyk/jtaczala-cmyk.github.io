Cat Leap (Kattesprang) · free jumping game

Privacy

Last updated 27.11.2026 · Norsk · Polski

In short

Cat Leap has no accounts, no ads and no profiling, and we sell no data. The game uses no cookies.

Controller

Jacek Taczala (a private person who made the game, free for everyone), contact: kontakt@stop60.no.

Data on your device

Your browser storage (localStorage) on this device keeps your best score and your sound choice (keys s60_kattesprang_best and s60_kattesprang_mute). These are not cookies and do not leave the device.

Anonymous statistics (GoatCounter)

We count visits and the events “game started”, “game over” and “score shared/copied” with GoatCounter, without cookies. IP address and browser are used only in server memory (up to 8 hours) to count unique visits; only totals are stored. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). You can opt out: block scripts, or open the game with #toggle-goatcounter at the end of the address.

Recipients

GoatCounter (statistics, servers in the EU). GitHub, Inc. (USA) – hosting via GitHub Pages; GitHub may log IP address and browser data when pages load (EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

Your rights

You have the right of access, correction, deletion, restriction and to object. Write to kontakt@stop60.no. You can complain to Datatilsynet or UODO.
